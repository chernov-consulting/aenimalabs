01

02

03

First mission — active

Kodama

an Ænima Labs venture

A trusted friend who carries your story.

Kodama is a consented identity-continuity service. A person, while alive, is guided through a structured capture process — their knowledge, values, reasoning, and way of seeing the world. The output is not an impersonation.

It is a trusted friend of the donor: a conversational counterpart that speaks about them with warmth and authority to the people who come after. The donor authors their own continuity. Their heirs receive the right to access — and the right to refuse.

Kodama (木霊) is the Japanese word for a spirit that dwells in an ancient tree, and for the echo it sends back through the forest. Rooted and patient. Present without possessing.
